## Tactical Tips for Hard-Mode Encounters

When playing on harder difficulty tiers, general gameplay fundamentals become much stricter. Keep the following operational principles in mind during challenging stretches:

* **Stagger Defensive Cooldowns:** Never pop defensive skills simultaneously. Cycle Alistair’s shield wall followed by Elodie’s damage ward to maintain perpetual damage reduction.
* **Capitalize on Status Combos:** Apply vulnerability debuffs using Mira before firing off Seraphina’s high-cost arcane skills to maximize burst damage.
* **Manage Initiative Turn Orders:** Use equipment bonuses to make sure your debuffers take their turns before your primary damage dealers.
* **Monitor Mental Resolve:** If an ally's Resolve gauge drops below 25%, rotate them out of direct harm's way to avoid panic and friendly fire penalties.

Adapting these habits transforms tough battles into methodical victories, letting you focus on the overarching narrative and companion storylines.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which Bless You Again characters are best for beginners?
For new players, running a foundational lineup consisting of Alistair Vance, Elodie Laurent, Seraphina Holt, and Kaelen Morse offers the safest learning curve. Alistair absorbs high punishment, Elodie provides broad healing margins, Seraphina delivers reliable area clearing, and Kaelen eliminates dangerous single targets quickly without complex setup requirements.

### How do I unlock all secret Bless You Again characters?
Unlocking hidden companions requires reaching specific alignment checkpoints during Chapters 3 and 5. Avoid antagonizing neutral NPC factions during exploration, complete all optional investigative side quests, and maintain high disposition scores with your core squad to trigger the necessary recruitment events.

### Can companions leave the party permanently based on choices?
Yes. Certain critical dialogue choices directly challenge the core moral codes of specific allies. Ignoring a companion's explicit warnings during major faction conflicts or failing their loyalty questlines can cause them to permanently abandon the party or turn hostile in subsequent chapters.

### Does character positioning matter in tactical combat?
Positioning plays a major role in combat effectiveness. Frontline companions intercept direct physical charges and draw single-target aggro, whereas midline and backline units remain protected from heavy melee cleaves, allowing them to channel powerful spells and support abilities undisturbed.
