## Frequently Asked Questions

### What makes the Bless You Again homunculus unique compared to standard companions?
The **Bless You Again homunculus** possesses a hybrid biological-construct template, granting complete immunity to natural poisons, bleeds, and panic afflictions. Furthermore, its ability to scale hybrid physical and arcane damage makes it the most flexible modular character in your roster.

### Can the homunculus permanently die or break during high-difficulty runs?
In standard play, the construct simply collapses into an inert state when integrity drops to zero, requiring field repair kits or restorative spells to reactivate. On permadeath or hardcore difficulty settings, however, community reports confirm that losing the construct permanently destroys its memory matrix, locking you out of its dedicated narrative arc.

### Which early-game stat investment yields the best return for the homunculus?
Investing in Transmutation Force alongside modest bumps to Vital Integrity provides the smoothest progression. This ensures the construct deals solid hybrid damage with basic skills while maintaining enough survivability to withstand aggressive early-game elite encounters.

### Does dialogue choice affect the combat capabilities of the homunculus?
Yes. How you treat your companion during campfire rests and story milestones influences its self-realization rating. Fostering independence unlocks higher critical strike multipliers and aggressive traits, while emphasizing steadfast loyalty bolsters its damage mitigation and defensive shielding capabilities.
