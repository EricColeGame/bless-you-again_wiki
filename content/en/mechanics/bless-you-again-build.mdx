## Core Mechanics Behind the Bless You Again Build

At its core, this setup revolves around a self-reinforcing chain reaction. Rather than relying on static protection, the archetype capitalizes on cyclical trigger loops: when you take lethal damage, drop below critical health thresholds, or consume a divine charge, the central blessing automatically cascades into a secondary defensive and offensive resurgence.

Understanding the internal cooldown (ICD) structure of your primary trigger is paramount. Community reports and player experience show that neglecting ICD thresholds leads to "dead windows"—intervals where incoming burst damage exceeds your recovery window. By aligning renewal traits with damage-reduction mitigation frames, your character gains functional invulnerability cycles paired with stacking retributive damage multipliers.

The table below outlines how the primary mechanical layers interact to turn potential defeat into massive burst windows.

| Mechanic Layer | Primary Function | Interaction with Core Trait | Recommended Internal Cooldown |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Resurgence Trigger** | Prevents lethal blows and restores baseline vitality | Resets cooldown on secondary bless procs | 45s – 60s base (scale lower) |
| **Recursive Aura** | Emits radiant area damage upon trigger activation | Converts absorbed damage into offensive scaling | Continuous / 0s ICD |
| **Cleansing Wave** | Strips debuffs, damage-over-time, and control effects | Activates instantly when low-health threshold is crossed | 15s ICD |
| **Sanctified Bastion** | Grants temporary barrier proportional to maximum mana | Multiplies incoming healing efficiency by 35% | Synchronized with Resurgence |

When playing this setup, timing your aggression around your safety nets provides unprecedented tempo control. Instead of retreating when health dips, players can deliberately surf low-health thresholds to maximize their damage output while trusting the safety protocols inherent to the design.

## Stat Priority and Threshold Benchmarks

Building an effective character requires strict adherence to stat priority tiers. Because the central passive relies heavily on cycling secondary procs, Cooldown Reduction (CDR) and Proc Efficiency take precedence over flat offensive stats. Without sufficient CDR, your survival loop degrades during multi-phase boss encounters.

Once you satisfy the baseline cooldown metrics, shift your investment into Effective Health Pool (EHP) multipliers. Health and mitigation stats amplify the sheer quantity of effective hit points granted by your trigger bursts, turning narrow escapes into fortified counterattacks.

| Stat Name | Priority Tier | Recommended Target | Mechanical Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cooldown Reduction (CDR)** | Tier 1 (Mandatory) | 38% – 45% minimum | Lowers trigger intervals and tightens protective uptime |
| **Proc Efficiency / Divine Flow** | Tier 1 (Mandatory) | +50% baseline | Guarantees consistent secondary burst chains |
| **Damage Mitigation / Armor** | Tier 2 (High) | 65% total mitigation | Prevents one-shot spikes that bypass the activation check |
| **Critical Strike Multiplier** | Tier 2 (High) | 220%+ | Amplifies counter-burst damage following a trigger event |
| **Maximum Resource Pool** | Tier 3 (Moderate) | +25% base reserve | Expands the absorption ceiling of defensive barriers |
| **Flat Movement Speed** | Tier 3 (Utility) | +15% – 20% | Aids repositioning during post-trigger invulnerability frames |

Balancing these stats prevents common pitfalls. Investing solely into damage leaves your character brittle if a boss strings together consecutive untelegraphed hits, whereas over-investing in pure tankiness diminishes the counter-offensive power that makes this playstyle formidable.
