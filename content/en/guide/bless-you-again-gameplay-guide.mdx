## Essential Survival Tips and Common Pitfalls

Even skilled veterans can fall victim to unexpected traps or sudden damage spikes. Keeping these practical guidelines in mind will immediately improve your survivability across every tier of play:

* **Respect Stamina Limits:** Never drain your entire stamina gauge on offensive attacks. Always reserve enough stamina for at least one defensive dash to escape counter-blows.
* **Observe Environmental Hazards:** Arenas frequently feature collapsing terrain, spikes, or flame vents. Use knockback skills to push durable enemies into environmental hazards for free elimination.
* **Do Not Reroll Recklessly:** Save blessing rerolls for late-stage shops or critical boss-reward screens when searching for specific keystone synergies.
* **Prioritize Cooldown Reduction:** Offensive skills that refresh quickly provide significantly more utility and crowd-control uptime than slow, heavy-hitting abilities.
* **Learn Boss Reset Windows:** Every major boss features a recovery window following their signature combination. Use this brief pause to heal or unleash your most powerful combo.

Applying the recommendations in this Bless You Again gameplay guide will steadily turn difficult runs into manageable victories, giving you the confidence to push deeper into endgame trials.
