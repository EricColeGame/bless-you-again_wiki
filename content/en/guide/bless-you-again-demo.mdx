3. **Retrieve the Hallway Key:** Open the lower drawer of the nightstand. Community reports highlight that players often overlook this drawer because its handle blends subtly into the woodwork.

### Phase 2: Hallway Navigation and Environmental Clues

Upon unlocking the door, you enter a dim corridor where sound design becomes your primary guide:

- **Listen Carefully:** Wear high-quality headphones. Footsteps, wind draft shifts, and subtle tonal hums indicate which doors hold active interactions.

### Quick Settings Optimization Checklist

If you notice intermittent frame drops while testing the build, try these quick adjustments:

* **Lower Volumetric Fog:** Drop fog quality from High to Medium; this typically recovers 8–12 FPS in interior rooms without noticeable visual compromise.
* **Cap Frame Rate:** Set an in-engine or driver-level limit at 60 FPS to prevent erratic frame pacing.
* **Disable Motion Blur:** Turning off camera blur sharpens textual details on inspectable items and notes.
* **Adjust V-Sync:** If using a high-refresh monitor with adaptive sync (G-Sync or FreeSync), disable V-Sync in-game and enable it globally in your GPU control panel.
