## Overview of Combat Disciplines and Ability Types

Combat revolves around a fluid system of divine blessings, physical martial arts, and elemental incantations. Every ability in the game falls into a designated discipline designed to address specific battlefield conditions. Recognizing how these disciplines complement one another helps you assemble a versatile loadout rather than relying on a one-dimensional playstyle.

Understanding how each discipline scales ensures you invest your skill points and equipment attributes efficiently. The core disciplines divide into four primary categories:

*   **Vanguard Assault:** Direct melee strikes and burst damage capabilities engineered to shatter enemy posture.
*   **Radiant Ward:** Defensive barriers, counter-parries, and invulnerability windows designed for damage mitigation.
*   **Aetherial Channeling:** Ranged projectiles, crowd control zones, and elemental damage over time.
*   **Grace Restoration:** Health recovery, cleanse effects, and passive resource replenishment.

## Resource Management and Combat Rotation

A common mistake among newer players is exhausting their resources through uncalculated ability spamming. To maximize the efficiency of your **Bless You Again skills**, you must master rotation pacing and resource cycling.

Every active maneuver consumes either Stamina, Mana, or Focus. Depleting your stamina leaves you incapable of dodging, while dry mana pools leave you with no defensive answers to incoming area-of-effect spells.

```
Combat Rotation Priority:
1. Open with Mobility / Positioning Skill (Hallowed Step)
2. Deploy Crowd Control or Stagger Opener (Sunburst Thrust / Judgment Gale)
3. Execute Primary Damage Channel (Celestial Torrent / Sanctified Cleave)
4. Hold Reactionary Mitigation (Aegis of the Penitent) for Boss Counterattacks
5. Reset Position & Regenerate Resources
```

Maintaining this rhythm prevents catastrophic stamina lockouts. Treat your defensive abilities as reactionary insurance rather than proactive buffs, ensuring they remain off cooldown when heavy damage windows occur.

## Tactical Tips for Boss Battles and High-Level Encounters

Boss fights in this combat system punish greedy behavior. Rather than attempting to chain prolonged combos, adapt your usage of **Bless You Again skills** to the telegraphs and attack cadences of each opponent.

### 1. Observe Recovery Windows Before Casting
Heavy skills like *Celestial Torrent* feature a brief wind-up animation. Initiating this ability while a boss is in a neutral state often results in your attack getting interrupted by a sudden dash or projectile. Always wait until the enemy concludes a major combo string before committing to long animations.

### 2. Leverage Invulnerability Frames Over Pure Distance
Dodging away often leaves you out of range to retaliate once the boss finishes an attack. Incorporating abilities like *Hallowed Step* allows you to dodge directly through sweeping strikes, placing you behind the target in prime position to execute *Sunburst Thrust* for immediate stagger accumulation.

### 3. Cycle Defensive Cooldowns Deliberately
Never stack multiple defensive buffs simultaneously. If you activate *Aegis of the Penitent*, save your mobility dodges and healing surges for the subsequent attack cycle. Layering defensive cooldowns unnecessarily creates massive periods of vulnerability later in the encounter.

### 4. Adjust Loadouts Between Phases
Many elite encounters feature dramatic shifts between combat phases, swapping from slow, heavy physical attacks to rapid elemental barrages. Player experience shows that altering your loadout between checkpoint attempts to prioritize mobility over static wards significantly increases survival rates against volatile bosses.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which Bless You Again skills should beginners unlock first?
Beginners should immediately prioritize *Hallowed Step* and *Sanctified Cleave*. *Hallowed Step* provides a forgiving escape tool with generous invulnerability frames, while *Sanctified Cleave* offers wide-arc damage that easily handles clustered enemies without requiring precise aiming.

### Can I re-specialize my Bless You Again skills if I want to switch builds?
Yes. Skill respecs are available at central resting sanctuaries. While early-game respecs may require minor currency sacrifices, late-game progression gives players ample flexibility to reallocate points into different disciplines to suit specific boss encounters.

### How do status ailments impact skill damage scaling?
Certain abilities inflict elemental or divine afflictions such as Burn, Stun, or Holy Smite. When an enemy is afflicted, matching skills deal multiplied stagger and critical damage. Harmonizing your active abilities with passive affliction multipliers is key to maximizing overall damage per second.

### What is the most effective way to counter unblockable attacks?
Unblockable attacks are designated by distinct visual cues and cannot be mitigated by standard shielding. The most consistent counter is using invulnerability-frame abilities like *Hallowed Step* to slip through the attack's hitbox, or deploying *Aegis of the Penitent* to negate the damage entirely.
