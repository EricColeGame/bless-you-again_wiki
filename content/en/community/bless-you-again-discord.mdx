## Best Practices for LFG and Matchmaking in Bless You Again

One of the greatest benefits of the **Bless You Again Discord** is cutting down queue times and avoiding uncommunicative random teammates. However, posting vague requests like "need team" often leads to wasted time and mismatched expectations.

Player experience shows that structured recruitment messages yield much higher success rates. When creating a recruitment post, specify your timezone, current progression tier, preferred communication style (voice chat versus ping-only), and target activity.

```
RECOMMENDED LFG POST FORMAT:
[Activity / Dungeon Name] | [Region: NA/EU/Asia] | [Difficulty Tier]
Need: 1 Support, 2 DPS | Voice: Optional (Discord VC #4)
Goal: Fast daily clear | Drop tag or join voice directly!
```

To ensure smooth cooperative sessions, review the operational checklist below before jumping into voice channels:

| Planning Step | Recommended Action | Common Mistake to Avoid |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Regional Alignment** | Verify all group members share a compatible data center. | Grouping with cross-region players resulting in 250+ ms latency. |
| **2. Role Definition** | State required team roles clearly in the recruitment post. | Starting an encounter with duplicate roles and zero healing or defense. |
| **3. Voice Etiquette** | Test microphone input levels and enable push-to-talk in busy lobbies. | Leaving open microphones active with background static or music. |
| **4. Experience Level** | Clarify whether the group is "blind learning" or "experienced speedrun." | Expecting instant execution from players who are learning mechanics. |
| **5. Post-Run Wrap-up** | Leave the voice channel promptly once your session concludes. | Idling in active LFG voice lobbies, blocking other squads from using them. |

Using the dedicated voice lobbies effectively transforms difficult encounters into coordinated, enjoyable clears. If your party wipes, voice communication makes it simple to analyze mistakes and adjust strategy on the fly.

## Community Etiquette, Moderation Rules, and Safety Tips

A large gaming Discord requires clear behavioral guidelines to remain safe and productive for everyone. Familiarizing yourself with community expectations safeguards your account against moderation timeouts or bans.

The server enforces zero tolerance for harassment, hate speech, unsanctioned real-money trading (RMT), and game exploitation discussions. Always keep discussions respectful, particularly in competitive balance threads where opinions can differ sharply.

### Protecting Your Account from Direct Message Scams
Discord accounts in active gaming servers are frequent targets for phishing campaigns and malicious bot networks. Malicious actors frequently disguise themselves as server staff, contest managers, or automated balance bots offering complimentary beta keys or free Discord Nitro.

*   **Turn off Direct Messages from Server Members:** In your server privacy settings, you can toggle off incoming DMs from users who are not on your personal friends list.
*   **Never Click Shortened External Links:** Official announcements always come from verified administrative channels rather than unsolicited direct messages. For additional best practices on maintaining account security, consult the [Discord Safety Center guidelines](https://discord.com/safety) on identifying social engineering and phishing tactics.
*   **Report Suspicious Activity Privately:** If an account sends you unsolicited links or asks for login credentials, capture a screenshot and open a ticket with server moderators instead of engaging with the sender.

Review the following compliance matrix to understand how infractions are typically handled across the server:

| Infraction Type | Example Behavior | Standard Moderation Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Channel Misuse** | Posting LFG requests in lore or art channels | Message deletion & verbal redirect |
| **Spamming / Self-Promotion** | Advertising personal streaming links outside media channels | 24-hour timeout & link purge |
| **Harassment / Toxicity** | Insulting players over failed encounter mechanics | Tiered timeout leading to server ban |
| **Exploit Distribution** | Sharing unverified third-party cheats or automation software | Immediate permanent ban without warning |
| **Phishing / Account Compromise** | Distributing suspicious verification or nitro gift links | Immediate ban and report to trust teams |

Maintaining a courteous tone ensures that the community remains a welcoming destination for players across all skill tiers.
