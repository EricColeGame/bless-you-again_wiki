## Optimal Audio and Display Configurations

To truly appreciate the visual craft and subtle acoustic design of **Bless You Again**, taking a few moments to properly configure your console settings is critical. Many players overlook native display calibration and 3D audio configuration, which can lead to washed-out blacks or muddied spatial sound.

Follow these step-by-step best practices to configure your hardware for the best presentation:

### Audio Setup Guide

1. Navigate to **Settings > Sound > Audio Output** on your home screen.
2. Select your output device (Soundbar, AV Amplifier, or Controller-connected Headphones).
3. If using headphones, enable **3D Audio for Headphones** and run the ear-level acoustic calibration wizard.
4. For home theater setups, ensure the audio format prioritizes Linear PCM or bitstream multichannel audio to prevent processing latency.

### HDR Calibration Best Practices

Proper HDR calibration ensures that dark scenes maintain readable shadow detail without blowing out highlights:

| Step | Calibration Stage | Action Required | Recommended Outcome |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Step 1** | Adjust Maximum Luminance (Screen 1) | Press Up/Down until the symbol is barely visible against white | Peak display nit limit matched |
| **Step 2** | Adjust Clip Point (Screen 2) | Repeat adjustment until the icon just vanishes | Prevents highlight crushing |
| **Step 3** | Adjust Black Floor (Screen 3) | Set brightness until the icon merges with true black | Deep blacks without losing shadow detail |

Taking these steps before launching **Bless You Again PS5** guarantees that high-contrast narrative moments and atmospheric lighting behave exactly as intended by the art team.

## Performance Troubleshooting and Storage Management

Because modern games rely heavily on high-speed asset streaming, poor drive maintenance or inconsistent system states can occasionally lead to stutter or visual artifacts. While the PS5 architecture is remarkably stable, applying sound storage practices will maintain optimal system throughput.

### Managing Storage Footprint

Even narrative-centric games require generous scratch space for temporary caching and auto-save checkpoints. We recommend keeping at least 15% to 20% of your internal SSD storage free to allow the system software to handle dynamic virtual memory paging smoothly.

| Storage Drive Type | Sequential Read Target | Recommended Usage | Impact on Load Times |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Internal Custom SSD** | 5,500 MB/s (Raw) | Primary game installation and system cache | Sub-5-second scene transitions |
| **Compatible M.2 NVMe SSD** | 5,500+ MB/s (Recommended) | Expansion storage for active native PS5 titles | Indistinguishable from internal storage |
| **External USB 3.0 HDD/SSD** | Up to 500 MB/s | Archiving titles and cold storage | Not suitable for direct native PS5 playback |

### Quick Fixes for Common Console Issues

If you experience unexpected frame drops or glitchy behavior during extended play sessions with **Bless You Again PS5**, try these quick maintenance actions:

- **Rebuild System Database:** Turn off the console completely, hold the power button until you hear a second beep to enter Safe Mode, and select "Clear Cache and Rebuild Database." This resolves corrupted index records without erasing your save data.
- **Check for Background Downloads:** Active background game updates or media streaming apps can steal network bandwidth and CPU cycles, leading to momentary hitching.
- **Cycle HDMI Handshake:** If you notice flickering or black screens when toggling HDR, unplug and reseat your HDMI 2.1 cable, or toggle Deep Color Output in the video settings menu.

### How does the game utilize the PS5 SSD?

### What should I do if the controller haptics feel too intense?
You can adjust or tone down controller vibration strength at any time. Go to the console's **Settings > Accessories > Controller (General)** and change the Haptic Feedback Intensity and Adaptive Trigger Intensity to Medium, Weak, or Off based on your comfort level.
